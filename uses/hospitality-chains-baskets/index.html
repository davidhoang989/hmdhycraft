Handwoven Baskets for Hospitality Chains — Hotel, Resort & Spa Wholesale Supplier from Vietnam

Roll-out programmes for hotel groups, resort chains, and spa operators. Bathroom amenity trays, in-room storage, guest laundry hampers, spa product organisers. Fire-treatment on request, property-level packing lists, batch consistency across a multi-property roll-out.

A hospitality procurement manager evaluating a natural-fibre basket supplier is running against a very specific brief: consistent batch quality across every property in the group, fire-treatment compliance for the local jurisdiction, property-level packing lists so the FF&E consultant can hand a carton straight to each hotel, and a lifecycle that fits into the group's 24-month rolling refresh cycle. This page covers exactly that scope.

What hospitality buyers ask for on this category

  • Batch consistency across a multi-property roll-out. A 6-property group cannot accept visible weave or colour variance between the baskets in Property A and Property F. HMD produces the full roll-out in one production batch and only splits packing at the end, so every basket ships from the same weaving cooperative in the same colour lot.
  • Fire-retardant treatment where required. UK and EU hotel bathrooms and bedrooms are covered by BS 5852 (upholstery ignition source), and US hotels frequently require CAL 117 compliance. HMD applies a food-safe FR treatment on request; certificates travel with the shipping documents.
  • Property-level packing lists. Cartons labelled per property so the local logistics team can distribute from the central FF&E warehouse to each hotel without opening cartons to reconcile.
  • Logo application at the amenity level. Paper hangtag, leather patch, or laser-engraved wooden hangtag with the hotel or spa brand. Executed at packing rather than as a separate production step.
  • Replacement stock scheduled for the 24-month refresh cycle. HMD holds the design specs, colour references, and shape jigs on file for repeat runs so the second and third refresh cycles do not need to re-sample.

Fire-treatment note

Fire-retardant treatment is a chemistry choice, not a marketing box-tick. HMD applies the FR chemistry appropriate for the destination jurisdiction — a UK Premier Inn refresh gets a BS 5852-compliant treatment; a US IHG property gets CAL 117-compliant. The certificate travels with the shipping documents so the property's fire safety officer can file directly. Ask at RFQ stage which standard applies; HMD will not assume.

How a multi-property roll-out actually runs

A 6-property, 850-room chain roll-out for bathroom amenity trays plus in-room storage typically flows like this. Design and colour reference locked at PO stage after one physical sample round per SKU. Production runs across a single 8-week window at one cooperative to hold batch consistency. QC done in one pre-shipment inspection covering all 3,400 pieces. Packing split into 6 property-level sub-batches at the consolidation warehouse in HCMC, each with its own property packing list attached to the container documents. Container ships FOB HCMC to the buyer's nominated central FF&E warehouse, from where the buyer's local logistics team distributes to each property on the roll-out schedule.

The single production batch approach protects colour and weave consistency far better than a staggered production run would. If a chain wants to roll out to more properties later, HMD holds the design file and can produce a matching batch within 8 to 10 weeks; the shape jig and colour reference on file mean the second batch matches the first within the natural fibre variance band.

Spa and wellness sub-category

Spa product organisers and amenity presentation baskets are a growing sub-category with a slightly different design register from hotel bathroom pieces. The spa aesthetic typically calls for cleaner lines, tighter weave density (seagrass more often than water hyacinth), and lidded pieces that hide the internal amenity inventory. Common spa SKU list: shallow rectangular presentation trays for shampoo and body wash arrangement, lidded amenity boxes for slipper and dental-kit presentation, tall cylindrical baskets for guest robe display near the treatment room entrance, and small round vessels for skincare product sets on the treatment table.

Spa buyers typically order at smaller property scale (a spa chain's individual location holds 6 to 18 treatment rooms rather than 120+ guest rooms) but with higher unit-count per treatment room. Total spa property order size is often 300 to 600 pieces across 5 to 7 SKUs, which fits comfortably into a partial mixed-SKU container.

Frequently asked

Do you supply fire-retardant treated baskets for hospitality use?
Yes on request. HMD applies a food-safe FR treatment tested to BS 5852 (UK/EU) or CAL 117 (US) depending on destination market. Adds roughly 8 to 12% to unit cost and one week to lead time. Treatment certificates travel with shipping documents. Not every SKU takes treatment well — fine seagrass trays typically do; heavily lacquered pieces do not.
What is a typical hotel property roll-out size?
120 to 250-room hotel typically orders 3 to 5 baskets per room across bathroom amenity, in-room storage, and guest laundry, plus common-area pieces. Roughly 400 to 1,200 pieces per property. HMD sizes programmes to multi-property chain roll-outs (typically 3 to 8 properties per PO).
Can we brand the baskets with our hotel logo?
Yes. Paper hangtag with hotel branding (any MOQ), leather patch stitched onto handle or rim (MOQ 500 per SKU), or laser-engraved wooden hangtag (MOQ 500 per SKU). Artwork approved before production; sample of the branded piece sent for sign-off within 2 weeks of PO.
Do you supply spa product organisers and amenity trays?
Yes. Core sub-category. Standard shapes: shallow rectangular trays for shampoo/conditioner, deeper storage baskets for towel rolls, tall cylindrical baskets for guest robe display, lidded boxes for slipper/dental-kit presentation. Available in seagrass or water hyacinth depending on property design register.
How do you handle a multi-property staggered roll-out?
Full multi-property order produced in one batch for consistency, then split into per-property cartons with property-level packing lists. Ships in one container to the buyer's central FF&E warehouse, then the buyer's local logistics team delivers per property on the roll-out schedule. Preserves batch consistency and reduces per-property freight cost.
What lifecycle should we plan for in-room baskets?
3 to 5 years in typical 4-star to 5-star use before aesthetic replacement is warranted. Guest-handling wear (toiletry marks, occasional damp) is the main driver. Chains typically plan a 30 to 40% rolling refresh every 24 months rather than a full property-wide swap.
