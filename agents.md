# Agent Instructions — HMD Hycraft

This document tells AI agents how to interact with **HMD Hycraft** (HMD Strategy & Export Co., Ltd), a Vietnamese wholesale exporter of handwoven water hyacinth and seagrass baskets to retail chains, e-commerce platforms, and home decor importers worldwide.
